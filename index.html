Bobo's Castle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Bobo's Castle is a two-player cooperative game for Android. This page explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not sell or share anything about you.

What the game does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account is asked for.
  • No location, contacts, photos or files on your device are read.
  • No advertising identifiers are used. There are no ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are included.

Playing together online

To let two players reach each other, the game connects to a small relay server run by the developer. While you play:

  • Network address. The server sees the internet (IP) address of each device, because that is how data is sent back to it. Connection events (address and time) are written to a short technical log that is used only to fix problems and is overwritten automatically as it fills.
  • Game data. The positions and actions of the players are passed between the two devices through the server while the match is running. They are not stored.
  • Character choice. The character you pick (Mahmoud or Mays) is shown to the other player in your match. It is saved only on your own device.

Microphone and voice chat

The game asks for microphone permission only if you switch on the MIC button during a match. When it is on, your voice is sent live through the relay server to the other player in your match so you can talk to each other. Voice is not recorded and not stored, on the server or anywhere else. Switching MIC off, or denying the permission, stops this completely, and the game works without it.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers or other companies. Information is only disclosed if the law requires it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • You can play without the microphone.
  • Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your device.
